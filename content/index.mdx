## What's here

- **[PT1 Course](/pt1-course)** — 24 lessons across five modules, built around the
  real exam weights. Every command explained flag by flag, every wrong turn kept in.
- **[Command Reference](/commands)** — the tools and flags, with copy-paste-ready
  recipes and no target-specific data.
- **[Write-ups](/writeups)** — methodology notes from rooms and labs, written the
  way the exam wants findings written.

## How I use it

1. Learn something in a room or lab.
2. Add the command, with each flag explained, to the reference.
3. Write the finding up as if it were going in the report. That trains the graded
   deliverable for free, every time.

> These notes are for **authorised** learning, CTFs and lab environments only.
